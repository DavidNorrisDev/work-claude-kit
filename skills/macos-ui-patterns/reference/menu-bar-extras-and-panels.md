# Menu-bar extras, floating panels, and global summon

## 6. An app is a system, not a destination

The most native Mac apps don't make you go to them — they show up where you need them and get out of the way. This is the Spotlight / Raycast pattern.

- Consider menu-bar extras, floating panels, and a global keyboard shortcut to summon a quick-action window, rather than assuming the user opens a main window and stays there.
- A summoned panel should appear instantly, do its one job, and dismiss itself (Escape to dismiss, Enter to commit). Quiet entrance and exit.
- This suits capture-style flows especially; the main window remains the browse/manage surface.
- **Check:** does the app meet the user where they are, or force a trip to a window for quick actions?
